# CharFlut

Clean cuts. Bold prints.

CharFlut is a fashion label that sells print-on-demand T-shirts and hoodies to customers in the US and UK. The look is modern streetwear: a lot of white space, heavy black type, one confident yellow, and quiet grey. The clothes and the photos are the stars. The brand stays simple and gets out of the way.

## Colours

| Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Sun Yellow | #FFC800 | The one bright colour. Buttons, highlights, the logo tile. Use it sparingly so it stands out. |
| Black | #111111 | Headings, text, dark blocks, text on yellow. |
| Stone Grey | #8C8C8C | Borders, icons, big shapes. Not for small text. |
| White | #FFFFFF | Page background. Keep plenty of it. |
| Paper | #F4F4F1 | Product photo backgrounds, cards. |

Contrast checks (normal text needs 4.5:1):

- Black on Sun Yellow: about 12:1. Good.
- Black on white: about 19:1. Good.
- Muted text #595959 on white: about 7:1. Good.
- Stone Grey on white: about 3.4:1. Not enough for small text, so use it for lines and shapes only.
- White on Sun Yellow: about 1.6:1. Never do this.

## Fonts

- **Archivo, weights 800 to 900** for headings, buttons and the logo. Free on Google Fonts.
- **Inter, weights 400 to 700** for body text, prices and forms. Free on Google Fonts.

Short headings can be UPPERCASE with tight spacing, like a fashion magazine. Body text stays normal case.

## Voice

- Short, calm and confident. Say what it is, how it fits and what it costs.
- Style words: "clean", "bold", "oversized", "soft", "your own".
- No fake urgency, no loud sales language. Shipping times and prices are honest.

Tagline ideas (pick one): "Clean cuts. Bold prints." · "Wear your own statement." · "Made to be worn loud."

## Logo

The mark is a bold CF monogram in a yellow rounded tile. The wordmark is CHARFLUT in Archivo 900. See [logo-usage.md](logo-usage.md) for all versions and rules.

## Using the brand on the shop

- Layout: big photos, wide spacing, few elements. Think fashion lookbook, not catalogue.
- Header: logo-primary on white. Footer: logo-reversed on black.
- Buttons: yellow fill, black text, radius 8px. Hover: black fill, yellow text.
- Product photos on Paper (#F4F4F1) or white. Model photos full width.
- Use yellow for one thing per screen, such as the main button or a "New" tag.
- Garment print ideas: the CF monogram small on the chest, or the stacked logo on the back.
