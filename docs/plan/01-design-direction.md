# Design direction

The concept is **“From flat to fabric”**: every design starts as flat artwork and becomes a real garment as you scroll. This one idea links the 3D, the motion and the print-on-demand story, so the effects have a reason and are not just decoration.

### What we borrow from top fashion and product sites

These are inspiration sources for patterns, not designs to copy. Browse galleries like Awwwards, Godly and Siteinspire for current fashion sites before the design sprint.

| Pattern | Seen on sites like | How CharFlut uses it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Editorial lookbook layouts, big photos, few words | Aé Leon Dore, Jacquemus | Home and Drops pages read like a magazine, not a product grid |
| Interactive 3D product views | Nike product pages, Apple product pages | Rotate the tee or hoodie in 3D on the product page |
| Experimental, gallery-like brand worlds | Gentle Monster | One “wow” moment per page, never more |
| Oversized type, strict grid, black and white with one accent | Balenciaga, Off-White | Archivo 900 headlines, yellow used once per screen |
| Fast, simple checkout | Shopify Checkout, Apple Store | Checkout has no 3D and no heavy motion, only clarity |

### Signature moments (what makes CharFlut unique)

1. **The print pass.** In the home hero, a blank 3D tee floats in space. As you scroll, a yellow light line sweeps across it and “prints” the artwork onto the fabric (a shader reveal).
2. **Fabric that feels the scroll.** The garment ripples slightly when you scroll fast and settles when you stop, like real cloth.
3. **CF wipe transition.** Between pages, a yellow panel slides across with the CF monogram, then reveals the next page.
4. **Magnetic cursor.** On desktop, a small round cursor grows over products and shows “View”, and buttons pull gently toward it.
5. **Design drops as events.** Each new collection gets a countdown, a short story, and its own colour of the 3D light.

### Visual rules

- Colours: white and Paper (#F4F4F1) backgrounds, Black (#111111) text, Sun Yellow (#FFC800) for one main action per screen, Stone Grey (#8C8C8C) for lines only.
- Type: Archivo 900 for headlines (up to 160px on desktop, tight spacing), Inter 400–600 for body (16px minimum).
- Layout: 12-column grid, wide margins, at least 96px between sections on desktop.
- Photos: full-bleed model photos (edge to edge), product mockups on Paper, no busy backgrounds.
- Dark mode: the admin dashboard is dark; the store stays light, with dark sections for drama (hero, drops).
